Before you book

Does buying through you cost more?

No. You pay the insurer's premium for the plan you choose. I don't charge any fee. Policybazaar Insurance Brokers pays me a commission when a policy is issued.

Can you offer a discount or cashback?

No. Passing on part of a commission as a rebate is not allowed under the Insurance Act, so I don't do it. What I offer instead is time: understanding your situation, explaining the fine print, and helping at claim time.

Which insurers can you show me?

Plans from the many insurers available through Policybazaar. If what you need isn't available through me, I'll tell you so.

I already have cover from my office. Do I need more?

Office cover usually ends the day you leave the job, and it rarely covers your parents properly. On the call, we'll check whether you could manage without it.

Will you help if I have to make a claim?

Yes. I'll help you with the paperwork and follow up with the claims team. The final claim decision is always the insurer's.

Can you also tell me which mutual funds to buy?

No. Recommending specific funds or stocks needs a SEBI registration that I don't have. The insurance call stays on insurance.

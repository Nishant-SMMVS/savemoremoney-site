Save Money on International Hotel Bookings - Dec 2022

Nishant Gupta · Dec 2022 · 2 min read

Written in Dec 2022. Some rules, rates or products have changed since; please double-check the details.

Its holiday season and many of us will be planning vacation trips. And we all know how tiring and confusing it is to search for the best offer to get maximum discounts on your booking. So If you are among those planning a trip abroad (either now or in summers), then I have something of use for you. I have collated all the discounts being offered by different banks to help you save money International Hotel bookings for different days of the week. Most of these are applicable on credit cards but few are also applicable on debit cards.

I went through Make My Trip, Yatra and Cleartrip websites and scanned the currently running offers. From those, i picked the best discount offers applicable on different credit cards and different days. Idea was to have atleast one offer each day and have offers on different credit card.

As a result, we were able to cover maximum cards - Amex, Citi, SBI, HSBC, Yes bank, ICICI, Federal Bank, AU Bank, Canara Bank, IDFC, Bank of Baroda (BoB), Punjab National Bank (PNB)

Note - Most of these vouchers are applicable only till December 2022.

Disclaimer - I tried to pick best offers running. If any offer got missed, it maybe due to the offer not being good or it being oversight from me. So if you do not have the credit cards listed here, please do your own research before booking hotels :)
